Try it: interactive apps

Each app opens in a new tab and runs on a phone or laptop. None of them needs an account or an API key in demo mode.

ODI Grips customer-support chatbot

IBM 6450 · IBM 4212

A support bot for a real Riverside company, built from four prompt blocks: task, customer persona, tone and data access (the product catalog).

Try this: Ask “I want the Rogue in green.” Then open Structured Prompts, delete the Data Access block, and ask again. Does the bot invent a product?

CALIPER: build and check an AI text classifier

IBM 6450 · research

Write a definition, label about 30 examples, and let two AI coders and a judge turn their disagreements into written coding rules. The codebook is adopted only if it beats the plain definition.

Try this: Choose Walk through it step by step. No API key needed: it replays a recorded run (mean F1 .573 → .809 on 395 airline chat turns).

Simulated focus group

IBM 3302

Students rehearse moderating a focus group on a real client's product before running a real one. Co-designed with Dr. Fan Wang and student developers Anderson Edmond, Minh Nhat Doan and Yaza M. Tun.

Try this: Tick Demo (no AI) at the top left to open a recorded room of six personas, free and with no key.

Synthetic perceptual map (“Beat the AI”)

IBM 6450

Students replicate Li et al. (2024, Marketing Science): ask AI-simulated consumers to rate brands, map them, then check the map against a real class survey.

Try this: Guess where Starbucks, Dunkin' and Dutch Bros sit, then compare. The AI matched real students on 81% of brand-pair orderings (chance 50%; class n = 8).

Evidence

1,583students surveyed
413matched pre/post
20faculty adopters
40course sections
320hackathon students
  • Students gained on every AI self-assessment, pre → post: AI knowledge d = .62, confidence d = .51, proficiency d = .45 (all p < .001).
  • First-generation students gained the most self-efficacy (+0.65 vs. +0.35 for peers); 39.7% of matched students are Hispanic/Latino.
  • Honest note: the objective AI-literacy quiz did not move (d = .04). Next we assess skills, not only self-reports.
  • Hackathon: AI-literacy self-rating rose pre → post (d = .33, p = .006, n = 73 matched).
